# Credits

## Cat sprites

The cat is based on **[2D Pixel Art Cat Sprites](https://elthen.itch.io/2d-pixel-art-cat-sprites)**
by [Elthen's Pixel Art Shop](https://elthen.itch.io/), purchased and then
modified for this project: recoloured so the cat grades with each of the five
sky variants, and given the green eyes that glow at night.

The frames in `tools/anim_src/*.txt` are ASCII transcriptions of those poses —
one character per pixel — which is the form the firmware compiles. They are
derivative of the purchased artwork, not original art.

Elthen's itch.io page permits use in commercial and non-commercial projects and
points to a fuller licence. If that licence restricts redistributing the sprite
data itself, the ASCII frames in this repository are the thing to check: they
are a lossless representation of the art, published publicly here.
